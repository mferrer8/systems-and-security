# Chapter 2-17 – Exploring Alternative AI Sources Featuring Google Bard

**Status:** Completed  
**Focus:** Comparing alternative AI models for cybersecurity tasks

---

## Summary
This video explores using AI models other than ChatGPT, specifically Google Bard, for cybersecurity tasks. The instructor tests Bard on analyzing WordPress attack logs and checking CVE information, comparing its performance to ChatGPT.

---

## Key Takeaways

**Alternative AI Models**
- **Google Bard & Facebook Lambda**
  - Tested for cybersecurity-related tasks.
  - Observation: ChatGPT currently outperforms these alternatives for advanced incident analysis and log parsing.

**Practical Tests**
- **Log Analysis**
  - Prompt: Analyze raw WordPress attack logs and suggest next steps.
  - Result: Bard required rephrasing and still did not fully complete the request.
  - Conclusion: Not suitable for complex security incident analysis compared to ChatGPT.

- **CVE Lookup**
  - Prompt: Check a 2021 CVE.
  - Result: Bard provided a correct response for the CVE.
  - Note: ChatGPT-4 can provide the same or better results, with more consistent performance.

**Key Observations**
- For advanced cybersecurity analysis (incident response, log parsing, integration with SIEM tools), ChatGPT remains superior.  
- Bard may provide useful information for certain lookup tasks but is less reliable for complex analysis.  
- Demonstrates the importance of testing multiple AI sources but highlights ChatGPT’s current dominance in cybersecurity use cases.
