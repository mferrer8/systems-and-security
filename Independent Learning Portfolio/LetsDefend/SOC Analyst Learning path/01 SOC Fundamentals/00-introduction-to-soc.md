# Introduction to SOC

## Objective
Introduce the purpose, structure, and operation of a Security Operations Center (SOC) and outline the core tools and mindset required for a SOC analyst role.

---

## What Is a SOC?
A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a centralized team responsible for monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cybersecurity threats within an organization.

---

## Key Learning Goals
This lesson provides a high-level overview of:
- The structure of a SOC
- How a SOC operates on a daily basis
- Core SOC tools and products
- How SOC analysts should use their tools effectively
- Common mistakes made by SOC analysts

---

## SOC Structure & Operation
- SOCs operate continuously to monitor security events
- Analysts review alerts, investigate incidents, and escalate when necessary
- Processes are structured to ensure fast detection and response

---

## Core SOC Tools
SOC analysts rely on several foundational tools, including:
- **SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)** for log aggregation and alerting
- **Log Management systems** for visibility across systems and applications
- **EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response)** for endpoint-level threat detection
- Additional tools such as SOAR and threat intelligence feeds

---

## Analyst Mindset
- Tools are only effective when used correctly
- Analysts must be detail-oriented and methodical
- Taking structured notes improves investigation quality and learning speed
- Avoiding common mistakes is critical for effective incident response

---

## Who This Course Is For
- **Aspiring SOC Analysts:** Focus on learning SIEM, log analysis, and EDR fundamentals
- **Current SOC Analysts:** Review best practices, quick tips, and common analyst mistakes to improve efficiency

---

## Key Takeaways
- SOC Fundamentals is the required starting point for SOC careers
- Understanding SOC tools and workflows is more important than deep technical skills at this stage
- Consistent study and hands-on practice are essential for progress
