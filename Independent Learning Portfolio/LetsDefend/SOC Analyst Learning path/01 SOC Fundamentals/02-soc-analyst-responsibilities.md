# SOC Analyst Responsibilities

## Objective
Explain the role of a SOC analyst, where it fits within the SOC team, and the core responsibilities and skills required to perform the job effectively.

---

## What Is a SOC Analyst?
A SOC analyst is the first line of defense in detecting and investigating security threats within an organization. They are responsible for analyzing alerts, determining whether activity is malicious, and escalating incidents when necessary.

SOC analysts play a critical role because they are typically the **first responders** to security events.

---

## Core Responsibilities
- Monitor and analyze security alerts
- Investigate potential threats and anomalies
- Determine true positives vs false positives
- Escalate incidents when required
- Provide remediation recommendations

---

## Advantages of the Role
- Exposure to a wide variety of attack techniques
- Continuous learning due to evolving threats
- Less repetitive work compared to other IT roles
- Opportunity to build strong investigative and analytical skills

---

## A Day in the Life of a SOC Analyst
- Review alerts generated by the SIEM
- Correlate events using logs and endpoint data
- Use tools such as EDR, Log Management, and SOAR
- Determine impact and severity of incidents
- Document findings and escalate when necessary

---

## Required Skills and Knowledge

### Operating Systems
- Strong understanding of Windows and Linux behavior
- Ability to distinguish normal system activity from suspicious behavior
- Familiarity with common services and processes

### Networking
- Understanding of IP addresses, ports, and protocols
- Ability to analyze malicious IPs and URLs
- Awareness of potential data exfiltration methods
- Knowledge of network traffic patterns

### Malware Analysis
- Ability to identify and analyze malicious files
- Understanding malware behavior and evasion techniques
- Identification of command-and-control (C2) communication
- Verification of compromised hosts communicating with malicious infrastructure

---

## Key Takeaways
- SOC analysts are the first responders to security alerts
- Investigation accuracy is more important than tool dependency
- Strong fundamentals in operating systems, networking, and malware analysis are essential
- Technical depth will be developed progressively throughout the course
